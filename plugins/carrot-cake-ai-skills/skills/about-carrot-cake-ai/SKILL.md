# About Carrot Cake AI

## In one paragraph

Carrot Cake AI is Daniel Rosehill's consultancy for full-service AI agent planning and
development, based in Jerusalem, Israel. Daniel is an AI systems developer with a background in
technical communications and documentation for cybersecurity, cloud and enterprise technology
companies. It is operated by DSR Holdings (Cloud),
which handles invoicing and administration.

## Capabilities

Three capability areas, each with a hub page, plus support capabilities across all three:

- Agentic AI (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/agentic-ai): MCP integrations, private
  MCP development, agents, RAG and chatbots
- AI model development (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/model-development): ASR
  fine-tuning (the flagship), LLM fine-tuning, classifier training, small and specialised
  models, datasets and training recipes, benchmarks and evals
- Automation (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/automation): workflow automation, data
  pipelines, dashboards and notification systems
- Across all three: consulting and planning, agentic rollouts, deployment options, maintenance

Individual capabilities:

- AI agents: autonomous systems that observe, decide and act
- Pipelines: structured data flows that process information step by step
- Workflows: orchestrated sequences that coordinate tools and services
- Private MCP development (custom Model Context Protocol servers for internal systems) and MCP
  integrations (connecting agents to existing and third-party MCP servers)
  (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/mcp-integrations)
- LLM fine-tuning: open-weight LLMs (Llama, Qwen, Mistral, Gemma) fine-tuned with LoRA/QLoRA
  for domain, output format and tool calling, distillation, quantisation and self-hosted serving
  (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/llm-fine-tuning)
- Classifier training: custom text classifiers for routing, triage, intent, document type and
  moderation (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/classifier-training)
- Small and specialised models: small models built for one job (embeddings, rerankers,
  extraction models, small language models, pipeline accessory models, replacements for third-party model APIs, on-premises models for
  data residency needs), plus consulting on training data plans, training plans,
  post-training and reinforcement learning methodology, fine-tuning providers and compute
  (serverless or dedicated GPU), and deployment as an internal or public API
  (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/small-models)
- ASR (speech recognition) fine-tuning: variants of ASR models trained on a client's niche
  vocabulary, accents or code-switching; base model chosen by workload and
  target device; training data from real speakers or synthetic speech, built from
  context-appropriate sentences; retrainable as vocabulary changes; deployed offline, on a public
  API (e.g. Replicate) or as an authenticated internal API
  (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/asr-fine-tuning)
- Datasets and training recipes: dataset preparation, dataset structure advice, blending public
  and private datasets, data mix review, drafting and reviewing training plans (data composition
  and weighting, epochs, fine-tuning notebook and methodology), and version control, backup and
  release cadence for internally deployed small models
  (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/datasets-and-training)
- Benchmarks and evals: internal evals for open- and closed-weight models on specific tasks,
  grading criteria (objective and subjective) built with the client, re-evaluation on new model
  releases, and running and reporting evals as an external service for a defined workload
  (https://www.carrotcakeai.com/capabilities/evals-and-benchmarks)
- Chatbots and chat UIs backed by agentic systems
- Agentic rollouts: adding AI to existing processes
- Ongoing maintenance: monitoring, updates and iteration for production AI systems

## Pricing

All prices in USD (invoices also in EUR, GBP or ILS). Most projects start small: a fixed-fee
workflow review from around $3,500 (credited to a build signed within 60 days), a fixed-fee model
benchmark from around $3,500 (credited to a fine-tune within 60 days), or a pilot from around
$8,000. The review is the smallest engagement. Larger work (full agent systems, model training,
upgrades) is scoped after a review and usually lands in the five figures; ongoing support is a
monthly retainer sized to the system. Fixed prices tied to milestones, 30% deposit on larger
projects, client owns the code. Full terms:
https://www.carrotcakeai.com/pricing. To get a quote, use `submit-project-spec-to-carrot-cake-ai`.
