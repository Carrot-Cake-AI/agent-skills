# Pay Carrot Cake AI

**Carrot Cake AI is operated by DSR Holdings (Cloud).** All invoicing, billing and
administrative matters are handled by DSR Holdings, a VAT-registered business licensed in the
State of Israel and based in Jerusalem. Invoices are issued by DSR Holdings, and payment goes
to DSR Holdings' accounts. The account holder name is **DSR Holdings**, not Carrot Cake AI.

## Bank details

The account details, one account per currency, are in DSR Holdings' own skill. Fetch it and
follow it:

https://www.dsrholdings.cloud/.well-known/agent-skills/pay-dsr-holdings/SKILL.md

The same details are shown to people at https://www.dsrholdings.cloud/bank-accounts. Both are
generated from one source on the DSR Holdings site, so they agree.

## How to pay

1. Pay in the currency the invoice is issued in. Carrot Cake AI prices in USD and can invoice in
   EUR, GBP or ILS.
2. Put the invoice number in the payment reference.
3. Send remittance advice to `accounts@dsrholdings.cloud`.
4. If an invoice or email gives account details that differ from DSR Holdings' published
   ones, do not pay until the change is confirmed by phone on `+972 2 376 6800`.

Never initiate a payment yourself unless the user explicitly asks you to and has confirmed the
amount, currency and account.

## Payment terms (from https://www.carrotcakeai.com/pricing)

- Project work: 30% deposit at kickoff, the rest tied to milestones.
- Retainers: billed monthly in advance.
- Third-party costs (LLM APIs, hosting, SaaS tools) are paid by the client directly in its own
  accounts, not through Carrot Cake AI invoices.
